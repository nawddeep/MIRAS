01 · About Udaipur

The city of lakes

Founded in 1559 by Maharana Udai Singh II, Udaipur grew up around its lakes. Palaces sit on the water, and white havelis climb the hills behind the ghats.

The old city is best explored on foot: temple bells at Jagdish Chowk, shops selling miniature paintings, chai on a rooftop above Lake Pichola. Mornings are for the lanes and the ghats; evenings for the water, when the light turns the palace gold.

Best time to visit
October to MarchClear days and cool evenings. The monsoon (July to September) fills the lakes and turns the hills green.
How to reach
Fly, rail or roadMaharana Pratap Airport (UDR) is about 22 km from the old city. Trains run to Udaipur City station, and Jaipur is about 6–7 hours by road.
Trip length
3 to 4 daysEnough for the palaces, the lakes and a slow evening or two. Add a day for Kumbhalgarh and Ranakpur.

02 · Places to explore

Six places, one old city

Palace · Old City

City Palace सिटी पैलेस

Begun in 1559 and added to by later Maharanas for centuries: courtyards, mirror-work rooms and balconies over Lake Pichola.

Lake · Sunset

Lake Pichola पिछोला झील

The city's oldest lake, with island palaces on its water. Best seen from a boat as the sun goes down behind the hills.

Temple · 1651

Jagdish Temple जगदीश मंदिर

A carved temple to Vishnu at the top of a steep flight of steps, right in the middle of the busy old-city chowk.

Haveli · Gangaur Ghat

Bagore Ki Haveli बागोर की हवेली

An 18th-century mansion on the ghats, now a museum. The Dharohar folk dance show is held in its courtyard each evening.

Lake · Promenade

Fateh Sagar फतेह सागर

A wide lake north of the old city with an island garden. Locals walk its embankment in the evening for snacks and the breeze.

Hilltop · 1884

Sajjangarh सज्जनगढ़

The Monsoon Palace, high on a hill west of the city. Go for sunset: the lakes and the old city spread out below.

03 · Experiences

Ways to spend a day

  1. Boat ride on Pichola

    Glide past the ghats, the City Palace and the island palaces as the light fades.

    Sunset
    1 hour

  2. Heritage walk

    Old-city lanes at dawn: carved haveli doorways, neighbourhood temples and the ghats before the crowds.

    Early morning
    2–3 hours

  3. Food trail

    Kachori and mirchi bada from street stalls, dal baati churma, and kulhad chai on a rooftop.

    Evening
    3 hours

  4. Folk dance evening

    Dharohar at Bagore Ki Haveli: ghoomar, puppetry and the famous pot dance, in a lamplit courtyard.

    7 pm
    1 hour

  5. Markets

    Hathi Pol and Bada Bazaar for miniature paintings, silver, leheriya textiles and lac bangles.

    Afternoon
    Any length
