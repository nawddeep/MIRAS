No. 01 Udaipur, handed down

A city that is still being lived in.

Udaipur grew up around its lakes. Palaces sit on the water and white havelis climb the hills behind the ghats, but the city is not a museum. Washing dries on the ghat steps, a painter outlines an elephant with a single hair, and the chai is poured into clay cups.

MIRAS is a guide to that city. Find a place, read the story behind it with its source shown plainly, then meet the person down the lane who still makes things by hand. Where we are not sure, we say so.
