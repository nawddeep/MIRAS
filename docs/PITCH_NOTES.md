# Pitch notes: the roadmap page

Open `/roadmap` (the footer link "What comes next"). Everything below comes from that page and from `docs/PHASE6_NOT_BUILT.md`.

## The three sentences

1. "This is what exists." (Four prototypes: passport, memories, verified makers, hidden places.)
2. "This is what we refused to fake." (Five ideas show **Not planned yet**, and each says what must be true first.)
3. "This is what we will build when it can be done safely."

Then point at the line at the bottom of the page: "We would rather show less that is true."

## "Why no payments?"

"Because taking money needs four things we do not have: a licensed payment provider, verified businesses, a refund and dispute process, and a compliance review. Until all four exist, we add no booking or payment code. Today a visitor sends a maker an enquiry, the maker replies outside the app, and the app says no payment happens there."

## "Why no social features?"

"Because a social layer needs accounts, moderation, reporting and blocking, and a policy for minors, and none of those exist yet. What we built instead keeps people safe by default: the passport and the memories reel stay on the visitor's own device, and a hidden place a visitor suggests is not shown until a named moderator approves it."

## Be careful not to say

- Do not call anything "live" or "launched". The statuses on the page are the only wording to use.
- Memories: the photos are shrunk on the phone and sent to an AI model for captions, and are not stored on our server. Do not say they "never leave the phone".
- Passport stamps show which stories were opened on one device. They do not prove anyone visited a place.
- The makers, places and stories in the demo are samples and are marked as sample.
- The page is in English only. The Hindi version needs a native speaker (listed in `CONTENT_TODO.md`).
