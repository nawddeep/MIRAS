# Phase 6: bookings and payments (not built)

MIRAS does not take bookings or payments. Visitors contact artisans, workshop hosts and event organisers directly, outside the app.

This is deliberate. Before any booking or in-app payment can be built, all of the following must exist:

1. **A licensed payment provider**, with a signed agreement, for the countries visitors pay from.
2. **Verified businesses**: identity and bank details checked for every artisan, host or organiser who would receive money.
3. **A refund and dispute process**: who decides, how fast, and who pays when something goes wrong.
4. **A compliance review**: payments, consumer protection, tax and data protection rules that apply in India and to foreign visitors.

Until all four are in place, no booking or payment code should be added.
