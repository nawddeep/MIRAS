## M2 Discovery and place profiles: 2026-10-28 00:34

- Zod schemas for every entity (`lib/schemas.ts`). `history` stories require `sourceRef`, and `verifiedBy` requires `verifiedAt`.
- Repositories (`lib/repos/`): JSON content in `/content` is the base layer, with SQLite (`data/miras.db`) rows overriding by id for owner edits and onboarded listings.
- `lib/services/content.ts`: `isStoryVisible`, `getVisibleStories`, place visibility, filters and story search.
- Seed content: 3 sample places (Sajjangarh, Pichhola, City Palace), 3 sample stories each (one per source type), 3 sample artisans, 1 sample workshop, `content/media.json`. All `isSample: true` with generic "SAMPLE: … goes here" text and no facts. All places share one rough Udaipur coordinate.
- Placeholder media generated by `scripts/make-placeholders.mjs` (gradient SVGs plus one gradient equirectangular PNG). No third-party media.
- Home: search box and category chips (server-rendered, works without JS). Place profile: 360 viewer (Photo Sphere Viewer, loaded only on tap) or a photo labelled Illustrative, summary, opening hours, typical visit, tips and gallery.
- Files: lib/schemas.ts, lib/repos/*, lib/services/content.ts, content/**, public/media/placeholders/*, scripts/make-placeholders.mjs, components/Pano360.tsx, app/page.tsx, app/places/[slug]/page.tsx.
