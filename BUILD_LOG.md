## M5 Voice-first onboarding: 2026-10-28 00:52

- `lib/ai/`: `AiProvider` interface with a `mock` provider (default, every output labelled "Demo response") and a `gemini` provider (official @google/genai SDK, structured JSON output). Gemini is used only when both `GEMINI_API_KEY` and `GEMINI_MODEL` are set. The model name is never guessed.
- `lib/services/onboarding.ts`: `draftFromVoice` rejects empty audio without calling the model, validates the model JSON with Zod, retries once, then falls back to the typed form with whatever fields were valid. `createListing` always saves a draft first and publishes only on an explicit Publish tap, through the consent gate. If the gate refuses, the draft and its edit link are kept.
- `lib/storage.ts`: local storage provider (`data/uploads`) that records a MediaAsset with owner and permitted use for every upload, and refuses images flagged as showing people. Served via `/api/media/[name]`.
- `/onboard`: record up to 60 s (MediaRecorder), optional photo compressed in the browser to 1280 px JPEG, "Make my draft", then a review form with uncertain fields highlighted ("Please check"), a no-faces checkbox, a consent checkbox, "Save as draft" and "Publish". "Type instead" is always available. Messages cover a blocked or missing microphone, an unsupported browser, an empty recording, slow or no connection (upload timeout from `UPLOAD_TIMEOUT_MS`), and the model being unavailable. The final screen shows the private edit link.
- The Gemini prompt tells the model to use only what the speaker said or what the photo shows, and to flag `photo_shows_face`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/onboarding.test.ts` (11): valid JSON, retry after invalid JSON, fallback after two invalid outputs, empty audio, model failure, mock labelled demo, no publish without a tap, no publish without consent, faces refused, rights recorded. `tests/e2e/onboard.spec.ts` (3, Chromium at 360 px): fake-mic voice draft without typing, a denied microphone with the typed fallback publishing, and a slow network timing out with the recording kept.
- Files: lib/ai/{types,mock,gemini,index}.ts, lib/storage.ts, lib/services/onboarding.ts, lib/client/media.ts, app/api/onboard/{draft,submit}/route.ts, app/api/media/[name]/route.ts, app/onboard/page.tsx, components/OnboardFlow.tsx, lib/schemas.ts (headline, photo_shows_face), playwright.config.ts, tests/unit/onboarding.test.ts, tests/e2e/onboard.spec.ts.

## M6 Camera story mode: 2026-10-28 00:55

- `lib/services/camera.ts` `matchFrame`: the model gets the frame plus the supported places' ids and names only, never any story text, and returns `{placeId, confidence, personIsMainSubject}`, validated with Zod. Below `MATCH_THRESHOLD` (default 0.85), out of set, a person as the main subject, invalid output or a failed call all give "I don't know this one yet". A match shows that place's stories from `getVisibleStories` only. The frame buffer is zero-filled after the call and never written anywhere.
- `POST /api/camera/match` (3 MB limit, no-store). `/camera`: live camera with "What is this?", a "Choose a photo" fallback, a too-dark check in the browser before upload, and a camera-blocked message. In mock mode the visitor can pick a place by hand, labelled "Demo response". The server honours that pick only when the provider is the mock.

## 3.1 Memories (in-app highlight reel): 2026-10-28 10:20

- `AiProvider.pickMoments(frames, candidates)`: the mock returns "Demo response" captions with no place and no face claimed. The Gemini version's prompt limits captions to what is visible, with no history. Output is validated with Zod (`MomentPicks`, at most 5) and retried once.
- `lib/services/memories.ts`: the visitor's place choice wins over the model's suggestion (shown as "(suggested)"). Stories come only from `getVisibleStories`, and no story is shown when none is visible. Out-of-range or duplicate frames and unknown places are dropped. Frames are zeroed after the call.
- `POST /api/memories/moments`: up to 5 frames of 1.5 MB or less in one request, rate-limited (10 per 10 minutes), no-store, never written to storage.
- `/memories`: pick up to 5 photos or clips. One still frame each is taken in the browser (video: about 1 s in). Each has an optional place choice and a date stamp "(from your file)". A face flag forces a choice: cut, blur whole frame (stated plainly), or keep. The reel is in-app only ("not an exported video"), with captions, stamp, verified story with source label, and optional Synthetic voice narration. It is saved to IndexedDB on the device and can be deleted. No music.
- `content/music.json`: empty licensed-music slot. `content:check` fails on a track without a rights entry.
- Tests: `tests/unit/memories.test.ts` (9), `tests/e2e/tier1.spec.ts` (7, including accessibility, planner, mock reel save and delete, face choice, invalid output, and 360 px checks). 97 unit tests pass and 7 of 7 e2e pass.
